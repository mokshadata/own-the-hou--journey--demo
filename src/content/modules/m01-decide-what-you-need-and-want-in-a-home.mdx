Before you start touring homes, it’s important to take time to think about **what you truly need**, and how that may differ from what you want. Being clear about your priorities can help you stay focused, avoid feeling overwhelmed, and make stronger decisions when it’s time to make an offer.

Start by listing the things you **MUST HAVE** in your new home. These are the features your home absolutely needs to work for your life right now. They might include:

* A certain number of bedrooms or bathrooms
* An accessible layout
* A shorter distance to work, school, or family
* A home that does not require major repairs

Next, think about things that would be **NICE TO HAVE**. These are features that would be great if you can find them, but aren’t essential. Examples might include a large yard, a newer kitchen, extra storage space, or a specific architectural style.

It’s also helpful to think about **how long you plan to stay in the home**. A starter home that meets your needs for the next five years may look different from a home you hope to stay in long term. Try to balance your current lifestyle with some flexibility for the future.

As you make these lists, remember that **every home comes with trade-offs**. You may need to decide between location and size, condition and price, or features and affordability. Knowing your priorities ahead of time makes those trade-offs easier, and helps you avoid stretching your budget for features that don’t truly matter to you.

They can help you understand what’s realistic in your price range and keep you grounded as emotions come into play. A clear plan will help you focus your search, and give you confidence when making a final offer.
