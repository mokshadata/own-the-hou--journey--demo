In Houston and Harris County, severe weather is part of life. Hurricanes, flooding, extreme heat, and strong storms can all affect your home. Preparing your home for a potential disaster doesn’t have to be complicated, but it does require planning.

* **Review your insurance coverage annually**. Remember to review your homeowners insurance policy to make sure it would cover your home and your belongings in the event of a disaster. In Houston and Harris County, many homeowners will also have flood insurance, which is a separate policy and is important in areas with high risk of flooding. Even if you are not in a high flood risk area, you may want to consider purchasing flood insurance, because flooding can happen unexpectedly in severe weather. You can learn more about flood insurance at [Floodsmart.gov](http://Floodsmart.gov).
* **Keep important documents stored in a waterproof container, and make digital copies**. Important documents related to your home include insurance policies, your home deed, and mortgage information. Make digital copies or take photos of these documents on your phone, so you have copies in case the originals are damaged.
* **Photograph or take videos of your home and belongings** to document their condition before any damage occurs. This will help you demonstrate future damage to your insurance company if you need to repair or replace any items. 

For more information on disaster preparedness, check out the [Greater Houston Disaster Alliance](https://disasteralliance.org/be-prepared/) website for information on communication tools, building a preparedness kit, storing food and water, and more.
