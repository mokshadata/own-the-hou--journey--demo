The steps to buying and owning a home are:

1. Get ready by learning what to expect
2. Set your budget
3. Choose your homebuying team
4. Get pre-approved for a mortgage
5. Find a home and make an offer
6. Finalize your mortgage
7. Close on your home
8. Protect your home as a homeowner

Your steps and chapters on the left may be customized to your journey. To switch between all steps and your custom steps, toggle the "Explore all" switch at the top of the chapters list.
