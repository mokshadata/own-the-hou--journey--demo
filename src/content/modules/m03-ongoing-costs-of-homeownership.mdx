Owning a home means taking care of it-—and budgeting for ongoing expenses. Here are the costs you need to plan for every year that you own a home.

* **Maintenance and Repairs**: A good rule of thumb is to save at least **2% of your home’s value each year** for upkeep. That covers things like roof repairs, appliance replacement, or plumbing issues.
* **Insurance**: Homeowners insurance protects you from losses due to fire, storms, theft, or other damage. Homeowners insurance costs have been increasing each year in the Houston area, so you should leave some room in your budget to allow for increased costs of insurance over time. If you’re in a flood-prone area, you may also need separate flood insurance. Learn more about [flood risks in Houston and Harris County here]().
* **Property Taxes**: Taxes support local schools, roads, and services. If you are buying an existing home in Houston or Harris County, you can look up the home’s current tax bill at the [Harris Taxes Truth in Taxation database](https://harris.trueprodigy-taxtransparency.com/taxTransparency/propertySearch) to get a sense of what you may owe. However, home values are regularly reassessed, and your taxes will increase over time. If you are buying a new home in Houston, please know that your taxes are likely to increase by a large amount within a year of ownership, and you will need to plan for those future taxes as part of your monthly payments. Read [Own the HOU’s blog post on taxes](https://ownthehou.org/latest-updates/) for new construction homes in Houston to learn more.

They will also usually include payment for property taxes and insurance, which your lender will keep in an escrow account and pay on your behalf.
