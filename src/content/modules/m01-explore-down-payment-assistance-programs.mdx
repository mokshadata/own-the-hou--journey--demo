Saving for a down payment as well as closing costs can be challenging — especially for first-time buyers. The good news is that there are down payment assistance (DPA) programs in Houston and Harris County that can help make buying a home more affordable.

Down payment assistance programs can help cover part of your down payment, your closing costs, or both. Support may come in different forms, such as grants, forgivable loans, or deferred-payment loans. Each program has its own rules about how the assistance works and how long you must reside in the home post closing.

Because DPA programs are funded in different ways, your eligibility often depends on several factors, including:

* Your household income
* Where you plan to buy a home
* Whether you are a <Term term="first-time-homebuyer" mode="definer">**first-time homebuyer**</Term>

That’s why Own the HOU created this tool: to help you quickly explore active down payment assistance (DPA) programs in the Houston and Harris County area, and find out which ones you may qualify for based on your situation.

Use this [DPA program tool](https://harveyhomeconnect.tfaforms.net/340) to check eligibility and learn what steps to take next.
