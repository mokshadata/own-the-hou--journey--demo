The lender for your mortgage will do a thorough review of your income, debts, credit history, and other savings in order to calculate your "**<Term client:load term="pre-approval amount" mode="definer">pre-approval amount</Term>**", or the total loan value you can be approved for.

To learn more about how to choose a lender and get pre-approved for a mortgage, visit [Chapter 4: Get Pre-Approved for a Mortgage](/own-the-hou--journey--demo/chapters/c04-get-pre-approved-for-a-mortgage/)

When you receive a pre-approval, it is important to remember a few things:

* **Your Pre-Approval maybe not be affordable**: Pre-approvals often reflect a “stretch” budget – meaning they are the maximum amount that you could possibly take out in a home loan. But your maximum pre-approval amount may not match your desired, or truly affordable, budget for a home. 
* **Consider Other Financial Goals**: If you are saving for college, planning to start a family, or want to keep your housing expenses low so you can have more disposable income, you should look at your monthly budget and decide what you want your monthly payment to be.
