Now, you have an idea of what home price and what types of homes you may be able to afford. However, it’s also important to look at your monthly budget and understand how much you would prefer to spend on your home each month. This means looking at your savings goals and other financial goals, such as paying down debt. It also means looking at your usual spending each month and making sure you are OK with cutting your typical expenses if you will need that money for your mortgage payments instead. 

To make sure you’re comfortable with your budget, you have a few options:

* You can look up free budgeting apps and use them to create a simple budget.

  This will help you understand your monthly income and spending, and help you think about how these would change with a future house payment.

* You can take a Homebuyer Education Course with a HUD-certified Own the HOU homebuyer education partner, and get one-to-one housing counseling with their homeownership experts afterward.

  They will help you review your finances and understand how to make sure your budget for a home is on target.
