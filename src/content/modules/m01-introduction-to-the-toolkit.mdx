The Toolkit is organized into a list of chapters, which you can see on the left-hand side of the screen.

You can click on the <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="size-4"><rect width="18" height="18" x="3" y="3" rx="2" /><path d="M9 3v18" /></svg> icon at the top of the page next to the page title to show or hide the chapters list.

Each chapter covers a step of buying a home, with a final chapter on homeownership.

Each chapter has:

1. An introduction with an overview of the chapter
2. Pages with
    * Explanations
    * Videos to guide you
    * <Term client:load term="term" mode="definer">Terms</Term> you can hover over to see the definition
    * Tools such as interactive calculators and external resources
    * Printable checklists
3. A chapter review with that chapter's
    * Summary, and
    * <Term client:load term="term">Terms</Term>, videos, tools, and checklists in lists for quick reference

The menu at the bottom left of the chapter list has:

* "Home" - a way to get back to the main Own the HOU website, and
* "Contact Us" - a form to share your needs so we can connect you with a housing counselor

At the bottom of each page, you can click on the left button to go back a page, or the right button to go to the next page.
