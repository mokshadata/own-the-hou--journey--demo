For this calculator tool, you will need information including your annual income, your debts, and how much money you have saved for a down payment. You can also adjust other items that we have pre-filled, such as the interest rate, loan term, and insurance and tax costs. 

Once you fill in the items below, the calculator will display your estimated affordable home price and monthly payment; your estimated payment in five years with insurance and tax increases; and your estimated repair budget each year. You should compare these to your desired monthly budget for payments on your home to ensure that this home price is right for you.
