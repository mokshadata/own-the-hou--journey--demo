A good <Term term="realtor" mode="definer">realtor</Term> can make the homebuying process less stressful and more successful—especially if you’re buying a home for the first time. Realtors help you search for homes, understand neighborhoods and pricing, schedule showings, and guide you through making an offer. They also help you negotiate and coordinate key steps like inspections, repairs, and closing timelines. In short: your realtor is your guide and advocate during the home search and offer process.

### What to Ask When Choosing a Realtor

When choosing a realtor, look for someone who understands:

1. Your Housing Needs
2. Your Purchasing Budget
3. The Houston/Harris County market 

Some realtors specialize in working with first-time buyers or buyers using down payment assistance programs. This can serve as a big advantage for you, so look for realtors that have the Texas Affordable Housing Specialist designation. When engaging a realtor, you should feel comfortable asking questions. A good realtor will be patient, responsive, and clear about what to expect at each step.
