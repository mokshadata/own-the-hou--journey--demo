As you’ll see in our video above, a lender, or loan officer, is one of the most important members of your homebuying team. <Term term="lenders" mode="definer">**Lenders**</Term> provide the mortgage loan that makes it possible to buy a home. They help you understand how much you may be able to borrow based on your income, debt, credit, and savings. They will collect documents, review your financial information, and guide your loan through the approval process.

When you’re ready to choose a lender, the next three sections of our toolkit will help you go through that process:

* **Finding Financial Help to Buy a Home**: This section reviews <Term term="down payment assistance" mode="definer">**Down Payment Assistance (DPA)**</Term> programs that are available in the Houston area. If you are interested in using this kind of financial help to buy a home, some of these DPA programs only work with certain lenders. You should review the requirements of the assistance programs first before choosing your lender.
* **Overview of Important Loan Terms**: In this section, you will review important aspects to consider when you are choosing a lender and a mortgage loan. Reviewing this section will help you decide whether a lender is offering loans that are right for you.
* **Shopping for Loans and Finding a Lender**: This section will go over how to shop for loans by understanding what types of loans you may be eligible for, what their terms are, and how you can use online resources or mortgage brokers to navigate your options.
