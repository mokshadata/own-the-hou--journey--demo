If you’ve run the numbers and realized that the home you want is out of reach right now, don’t get discouraged. Many buyers start there. The good news is that there are several ways to improve your homebuying budget over time — and small changes can make a big difference.

1. **Strengthen Your Credit**
  <p>Your credit score plays a major role in how much you can borrow and the interest rate you’ll pay. A higher credit score usually means a lower interest rate, which can save you hundreds of dollars a month on your mortgage.</p><p>Start by checking your credit reports for free at [annualcreditreport.com](https://annualcreditreport.com). Read Own the HOU’s [Resources to Improve Your Credit](/own-the-hou--journey--demo/chapters/c03-what-s-your-budget/s04-understanding-your-credit/m02-resources-to-improve-your-credit/).</p>
1. **Reduce Your Debt**
  <p>Lenders look at your debt-to-income ratio — the amount of debt you owe compared to what you earn. Paying down car loans, student loans, or credit cards can lower that ratio and increase the amount a lender is willing to approve. Even small progress helps.</p>
1. **Save for a Larger Down Payment**
  <p>A bigger down payment reduces how much you need to borrow, which can make monthly payments more affordable and help you qualify for better mortgage terms. You can set a savings goal and build it gradually with automatic deposits or a separate savings account for your future home.</p>
1. **Explore Homebuyer Assistance Programs**
  <p>Don’t forget — you don’t have to do it alone. There are a number of local down payment and closing cost assistance programs for eligible buyers, and some lenders provide grants or low-interest loans to help bridge the gap.</p><p>Use our [Down Payment Assistance tool](https://forms.connectivetx.org/340) to see if you qualify for available down payment assistance programs in Houston or Harris County.</p>
