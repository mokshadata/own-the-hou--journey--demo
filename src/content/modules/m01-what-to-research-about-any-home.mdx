Once you find a home you like, it’s important to look beyond the listing photos and price. Where a home is located is important as it can affect your safety, expenses, and quality of life. 

### Flood Risk

Flooding is a major concern in this region, and it’s not limited to areas that are near bayous or other water sources. A home’s flood risk can affect insurance costs, repair needs, and long-term value. Before making an offer, it’s important to look up a property’s flood history and risk. You can do this using the [Harris County Flood Education Mapping Tool](https://www.harriscountyfemt.org/). Your realtor should also be helpful in providing flood risk information for each property you visit.

### Transportation Costs

Housing costs don’t stop at your mortgage. How far you live from work, school, childcare, and daily errands can significantly affect your transportation costs. Longer commutes often mean higher gas, car maintenance, or transit expenses, which can strain your budget even if the home itself seems affordable. Consider these added costs, and your daily commute times, before you make an offer on a home that is located far away from your job or your daily destinations. 

### School Quality

If you plan to send children to public school, researching various districts and school performance ratings can help you make a decision about whether the local schools are right for your children. When you’re looking at the website links below, remember that school boundaries can change, and ratings are only one piece of the picture on how a school will actually meet your children’s needs. You may also want to research local parent groups or school websites that can offer more information on a specific school. 

* Websites like the [Houston Association of Realtors (HAR) website](https://www.har.com/), [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/), [Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/), or [Homes.com](http://Homes.com) include details on which schools your children would attend based on the location of each for-sale home. You may have to scroll down in the home listing to find this information.
* While most home listings will include an assessment of school quality, you can learn more about any public school by searching its name in the [Texas Education Agency (TEA) school performance reports](https://txschools.gov/) website.
