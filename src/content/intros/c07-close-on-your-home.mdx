<Term client:load term="closing" mode="definer">**Closing**</Term> is the process of completing your mortgage loan and transferring ownership of a home to you, the buyer. In short, this means that your closing date is the day you become a homeowner!

**CONGRATULATIONS** on making it this far in your homeownership journey!

There’s a lot to do in the days leading up to closing, and it may seem overwhelming at times. Some things to expect are:

* **Putting money into <Term client:load term="escrow">escrow</Term>**, including the rest of your <Term client:load term="down payment">down payment</Term> and <Term client:load term="closing costs">closing costs</Term> that you haven’t already paid
* **Signing up and paying for homeowners’ insurance**, and potentially flood insurance if it’s required
* **Reviewing your closing documents**, including your <Term client:load term="closing disclosure">Closing Disclosure</Term>. You should get this at least three days before your closing date.
* Coming to closing day with your **identification** ready, and with your **funds** completely ready to close.
* Completing **important post-closing steps**, like filing your homestead exemption and considering how you want to legally pass your home to the next generation when it’s time.

Read the rest of the sub-sections in this chapter for a clear breakdown of these steps. This will help you understand what happens in the days leading up to your closing date, what to expect on closing day, and what to do after closing.
