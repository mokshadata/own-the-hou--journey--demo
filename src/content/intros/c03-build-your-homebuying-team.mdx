Nobody buys a home alone. Now that you've determined your budget, let's build your homebuying team!

In this chapter, we'll cover how to:

1. Start with a **housing counselor**
2. Find a **realtor**
3. Choose a **lender**
4. Find **financial help** to buy a home

so that you can build the right team for you to buy a home!

Once you have a team, we'll cover getting pre-approved for a mortgage.
