Whether you’re just starting to think about buying a home, you’re actively house-hunting, or you’re already a homeowner — you’re in the right place!

This Own the HOU Toolkit was created to help homebuyers and homeowners in Houston and Harris County navigate every stage of the journey. 

This toolkit is for you IF:

* You’re just beginning to think about buying a house
* You are already in the process of buying a house
* You tried to buy a house before and experienced setbacks
* You want tips on keeping your monthly costs of ownership manageable
* You already own a home and need help keeping it

This Welcome chapter covers

1. an introduction to the structure of the toolkit
2. an overview of the steps of buying and owning a home covered in the toolkit

Click "Introduction to the Toolkit" to learn more.
