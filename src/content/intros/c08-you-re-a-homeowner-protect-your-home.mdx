Homeownership is an ongoing commitment. Over time, your home will need maintenance, your insurance costs will change, property taxes will increase, and unexpected events like storms or job changes may affect your finances. The good news is that with planning and awareness, you can stay ahead of these changes.

Protecting your home means focusing on a few key areas:

* Keeping up with regular **maintenance** to avoid costly repairs
* Staying **prepared for disaster events** – and knowing where to find resources for disaster recovery if needed
* Maintaining affordable insurance coverage that keeps pace with changing risks
* Monitoring your property taxes and knowing how to protest your assessment
* Planning for the future, including how your home will pass to loved ones

Owning a home also means knowing when to **ask for help**. Many homeowners experience financial strain at some point, whether due to repairs, rising costs, or unexpected life events. Seeking assistance early can make the difference between staying stable and falling behind.

The video at the beginning of this section walks through practical steps you can take to protect your home and your investment. Think of it as a roadmap for homeownership in the years ahead.

The sections below break these homeownership topics down in more detail, with tools and resources specific to Houston and Harris County. Whether you’ve owned your home for a month or ten years, these steps can help you stay secure, informed, and in control.
