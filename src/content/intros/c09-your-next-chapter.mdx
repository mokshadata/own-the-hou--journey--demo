You made it through the toolkit — Congratulations!

Whether you started this journey thinking about buying your first home, in the middle of house-hunting, or already living in a home you own, you now have a map for what comes next. The work doesn't stop here, but you're not navigating alone.

## Where you've been

Across the eight chapters, you've covered the full arc of becoming and staying a homeowner in Houston and Harris County:

* Getting ready financially and emotionally
* Understanding your credit, budget, and savings goals
* Exploring mortgages, down payment assistance, and homebuyer education
* Finding the right home and the right team to help you get there
* Making an offer, navigating inspections, and closing with confidence
* Protecting and maintaining your home for the long haul

## Where you go from here

Homeownership isn't a finish line, it's a foundation. A few things to take with you:

1. **Keep learning**. Tax rules, insurance markets, and assistance programs change. Come back to this toolkit any time, and stay connected with Own the HOU partner organizations for ongoing classes and counseling.
2. **Build your team**. A trusted lender, realtor, insurance agent, and [HUD-Certified housing counselor](https://ownthehou.org/contact-us/) are the people who will help you as you make many decisions along the way. Start by connecting to a HUD-Certified Housing Counselor today.
3. **Care for the asset**. Set aside savings for repairs, review your insurance and property tax statements every year, and address small issues before they become expensive ones.
4. **Pay it forward**. When you're ready, share what you've learned with family, neighbors, and friends. Closing the homeownership gap in our community takes all of us.

## Stay connected

Own the HOU is a partnership of trusted nonprofit and government organizations working across Houston and Harris County. Whatever stage you're in, our partners are here to help — with classes, one-on-one counseling, down payment assistance, legal aid, and post-purchase support.

Visit [ownthehou.org](https://ownthehou.org) to find a partner, take a class, or talk to a counselor about your next step.

**Welcome to the rest of your homeownership journey. We're glad you're here.**
