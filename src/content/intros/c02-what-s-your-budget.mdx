In this chapter, we'll

1. Breakdown the **costs** to own a home, such as
    1. The **mortgage** or the monthly payments to pay off the full cost to own the home
    1. The **upfront costs** such as down payments and inspection
    1. The **ongoing costs** such as maintenance and taxes
1. Guide you to make an **estimate of your budget** for your home
1. Consider the estimated home budget in the context of your **overall monthly budget**
1. Talk about **credit**, why it matters, and how to improve it, and
1. Share tips to **increase your home budget**

After working on your home budget, you'll have the information you need to start building your homebuying team in **Choose Your Homebuying Team**.
