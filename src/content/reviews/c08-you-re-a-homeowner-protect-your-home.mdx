## Resources
###### Being Prepared for Disaster

* Learn more about flood insurance at [floodsmart.gov](http://Floodsmart.gov).
* [Greater Houston Disaster Alliance](https://disasteralliance.org/be-prepared/) website has information on communication tools, building a preparedness kit, storing food and water, and more.

###### Recovering From a Disaster

* [Connective Survey](https://www.connectivetx.org/what-we-do/storm-needs-survey/) documents community needs and connects people to resources, including home repair resources, as they become available.
* [211](https://www.211.org/), the United Way helpline, can help connect you to immediate resources like food and shelter.
* [FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual), a federal agency, has programs that help reimburse you for post-disaster expenses, and can provide resources for long-term housing if your home needs extensive repairs before you can return.

###### Property Taxes and Protesting Your Assessment

* If you believe your home has been overvalued or if any of these details are incorrect, you can [protest your assessment online](https://owners.hcad.org/publicArea/InstructionVideo.aspx).

###### Passing Your Home Down to the Next generation

* Learn how to fill out and record a <Term client:load term="transfer on death deed">Transfer on Death Deed</Term> (TODD) with The Earl Carl Institute's [step-by-step guide](https://www.tsulaw.edu/centers/ECI/Transfer-on-Death-Deed-Instructions-2023.pdf).

### Additional Homeowner Resources

<details>
  <summary><strong><em>Home Repair</em></strong>  (regular repair or disaster-related repair needs)</summary>
  <p>Both the City of Houston and Harris County offer home repair programs for their residents. Both the City and the County offer various levels of home repair services, and require the applicants are at or below 80% AMI (Area Median Income) and do not live in the floodway. Some non-profit organizations like Rebuilding Together also offer home repairs on a regular basis.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>City of Houston Home Repair: If you live in the City of Houston, the City offers multiple options for residents through their home repair programs. Applicants are required to be at or below 80% AMI (Area Median Income) and the property cannot be located in the floodway (find out here). <strong><em>Please note that funding is not always available as it is in high demand each year; call the City or visit their website to learn about current program status. </em></strong>To learn more, visit their <a href="https://www.houstontx.gov/housing/home-repair.html">website</a> or call (832) 394-6200.&nbsp;</li>
    <li>Harris County Home Repair: If you live in Harris County, the County offers multiple options for residents through their home repair programs. Applicants are required to be at or below 80% AMI (Area Median Income) and the property cannot be located in the 100-year floodplain or floodway (find out here). To learn more, visit their <a href="https://hcd.harriscountytx.gov/Residents/Home-Repair-Program">website</a> or call&nbsp; (832) 927-4957.</li>
    <li>Rebuilding Together: Rebuilding Together is a local nonprofit focused on helping to retain the character of Houston neighborhoods by keeping families in communities that they helped to build. They accomplish this through providing home repairs to local families to ensure homes are safe and can be passed from generation to generation. Their Community Revitalization home repair program is available to residents of East End (77003, 77011, 77012, 77023).and Third Ward (77004) who are at or below 80% AMI (Area Median Income). Apply <a href="https://rebuildingtogetherhouston.org/">online</a> or call (713) 659-2511 to learn more.</li>
    <li>Rebuilding Together Veteran Program: Rebuilding Together also has a Veteran Home Repair Program that is available to veterans in need of repairs to structural, roof, and electrical and plumbing systems, accessibility and safety modifications, and light exterior work. Apply <a href="https://rebuildingtogetherhouston.org/">online</a> or call (713) 659-2511 to learn more.&nbsp;</li>
    <li>Houston Habitat for Humanity’s Critical Home Repair Program: serves seniors, age 62and older, and veterans residing in Houston, TX, who earn at or below 80% AMI. The goal is to ensure homes are safe and resilient, so owners can remain in their homes and age in place for years to come.</li>
    <li>Houston Habitat’s Disaster Home Repair Program: restores homes damaged by natural disasters for homeowners earning at or below 80% AMI. By providing the repairs necessary to make homes safe, sanitary, and secure, including mold testing and remediation, we help preserve Houston’s dwindling stock of affordable housing and help existing homeowners remain in their homes and rebuild their lives.</li>
  </ul>

To find out if you qualify, visit <a href="http://www.tsulaw.edu/centers/ECI/">www.tsulaw.edu/centers/ECI/</a> or call (713) 313-1139.</li>
    <li>Lone Star Legal Aid: Lone Star Legal Aid is a nonprofit focused on protecting and advancing the civil legal rights of low-income Texans by providing advocacy, legal representation, and community education that ensures equal access to justice.
