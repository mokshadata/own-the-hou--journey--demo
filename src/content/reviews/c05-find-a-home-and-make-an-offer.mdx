## Resources
###### Tools to Research Homes You're Interested In

* **Flood risk** is a major concern in this region. [Harris County Flood Education Mapping Tool](https://www.harriscountyfemt.org/) is one way to look up a property's flood history and risk.
* Resources to research **school quality** include Houston Association of Realtors (HAR) website](https://www.har.com/), [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/), [Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/), [Homes.com](http://Homes.com), and [Texas Education Agency (TEA) school performance reports](https://txschools.gov/) website.
