In "What's Your Budget", you:

1. Learned the **costs** to own a home, such as
    1. The **mortgage** or the monthly payments to pay off the full cost to own the home
    1. The **upfront costs** such as down payments and inspection
    1. The **ongoing costs** such as maintenance and taxes
1. Made an **estimate of your budget** for your home
1. Reviewed your home budget in the context of your **overall monthly budget**
1. Learned about **credit**, why it matters, and how to improve it, and
1. Got tips for **increasing your home budget**

Next, you'll build your homebuying team in [Chapter 3](/own-the-hou--journey--demo/chapters/c03-build-your-homebuying-team/) and then, with your team and your estimates from this chapter, work on getting pre-approved for a mortgage in [Chapter 4](/own-the-hou--journey--demo/chapters/c04-get-pre-approved-for-a-mortgage/).
