In "Choose Your Homebuying Team", you learned that:

1. **Housing counselors** are homeownership experts who can help you understand the process from start to finish.

    They can review your credit, help you build a realistic budget, explain loan options in plain language, and help you create an action plan that fits your goals. Their job is to help you make informed decisions and move forward with confidence.

    They can help you regardless of where you are in your process and regardless of whether you end up deciding to buy a home soon or later.
2. **Realtors** help you search for homes, understand neighborhoods and pricing, schedule showings, and guide you through making an offer.

    They also help you negotiate and coordinate key steps like inspections, repairs, and closing timelines. Your realtor is your guide and advocate during the home search and offer process.

    A **buyer representation agreement** can help clearly outline and define the realtor's services, timeframe, and their **commission** and who will cover it.
3. **Lenders** provide the mortgage loan that makes it possible to buy a home. They help you understand how much you may be able to borrow based on your income, debt, credit, and savings.

    They will collect documents, review your financial information, and guide your loan through the approval process.

    If you are interested in using **down payment assistance (DPA)** to buy a home, some of these DPA programs only work with certain lenders. You should review the requirements of the assistance programs first before choosing your lender.


In [Chapter 4: Get Pre-Approved for a Mortage](/own-the-hou--journey--demo/chapters/c04-get-pre-approved-for-a-mortgage/), you'll learn important loan terms and get ready to shop for a loan.
